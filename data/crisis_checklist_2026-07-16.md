- **Two AI-adjacent complexes are now cracking simultaneously** — memory equities (−25% from Jun 25) and private credit (accelerating in the last 20 sessions) — while the index, public credit, and breadth stay serene. Either the market is absorbing sector rotations, or the periphery is failing first, 2007-style. This line is the whole game; re-score monthly.
- **Ledger precedent on this configuration:** conditions without catalysts can persist for years — CAPE first crossed its 1929 record in 1997, three years before the 2000 top. But every instance of the full structural set eventually resolved into a major bear, and margin debt at records meant the decline, when it came, was amplified by forced selling.
- **Post-war base rates:** bear = -33% avg over ~13 months; non-recession bears recovered in ~2 years; the current bull (from Oct 2022) is ~3.7 years old vs ~5-year average.
